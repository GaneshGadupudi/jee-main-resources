Frequently asked questions

What is JEE Preparation Platform?

A preparation platform that gives you a daily plan, daily practice, mock tests and analysis, so you always know what to work on next.

Is it free?

Creating an account and using the notes is free. Pricing for future features has not been decided.

Which exams does it support?

JEE Main and JEE Advanced first. Support for other exams may come later.

Why do I need to log in for Resources?

Logging in lets us keep your progress safe and improve the platform for active students. Every other page is open to everyone.

Are short notes enough to prepare?

No. Notes are for revision. You still need to learn concepts and practise questions.

How is the study plan made?

From clear rules using your exam date, remaining syllabus, weak chapters and daily study hours. It adjusts gradually after your tests.

Are the questions reliable?

Questions are checked by a person before they are published. AI-written drafts are never published without review.

Does the weekly challenge rank me against others?

Yes. You get a score, rank and percentile among participating students. Rank is one measure of progress, not the only one.

Is my data safe?

Your account is protected by login, and each student can only access their own data.

Is JEE Preparation Platform affiliated with NTA or the IITs?

No. It is independent. Always confirm exam dates, syllabus and rules on the official JEE websites.

I found a mistake. How do I report it?

Reporting options will be added soon. Meanwhile, contact the creator through the details on the About page.
