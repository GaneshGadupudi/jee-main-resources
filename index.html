Mock Tests & Error Analysis

  1. Take a timed test.
  2. Don’t look at solutions immediately.
  3. Calculate your score.
  4. Identify incorrect questions.
  5. Identify questions where you wasted time.
  6. Identify concepts that need revision.
  7. Reattempt the difficult questions.

How to Revise JEE

First revision: after a few days Second: after a few weeks Final: before the exam

Use short notes, formula lists, questions you marked and your error log. These gaps are a guide, not a universal rule — adjust to your own memory and time.

What Should I Revise First?

  • Your weak chapters
  • Topics you often get wrong in PYQs
  • Chapters you studied but forgot
  • Mistakes from mock tests
  • Chapters you haven’t finished

I don’t show chapter weightage as a guarantee; past patterns don’t fix future papers.

Choose Your Situation

I am starting JEE preparation

Learn the syllabus, pick one main book per subject, build concepts before speed, and make short notes from day one.

My syllabus is incomplete

List pending chapters, finish them in order of difficulty for you, and keep a small daily slot for revision so finished chapters don’t fade.

I have completed the syllabus

Alternate revision, PYQs and timed tests. Use your error log to decide what to revise.

My exam is very close

Stop starting new resources. Revise short notes and formulas, take timed tests, analyse errors, and protect your sleep.

I am preparing for JEE Advanced

It demands deeper concepts and higher-level problem solving. Spend more time on multi-concept problems and understanding why a method works.

Common JEE Preparation Mistakes

  • Collecting too many resources
  • Watching lectures without solving questions
  • Ignoring PYQs
  • Not revising
  • Taking mock tests without analysing them
  • Ignoring weak chapters
  • Constantly changing books or teachers
  • Comparing your preparation with others
  • Studying without a realistic plan
  • Neglecting sleep and health

    FAQ

    What is JEE Main / Advanced?

    JEE Main is the national exam for NITs, IIITs and other institutes and is also the qualifier for JEE Advanced, which is for IIT admissions. Check official sites for current rules.

    When should I start?

    Whenever you can build fundamentals steadily; many begin in Class 11.

    Are short notes enough?

    No. They are for revision; you still need concepts and practice.

    How important are PYQs?

    Very useful for understanding question style, but not the only practice.

    How should I use mock tests, revise, or prepare for Advanced?

    See the Tests, Revision and Situation sections above.
