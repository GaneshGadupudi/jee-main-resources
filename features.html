Features

One loop: plan, practice, test, analyze, improve. Here is what each part does.

📅

Personal study plan

Built from your exam date, syllabus progress, weak chapters and daily study hours. Tasks are marked Not Started, In Progress or Completed.

⏳

Exam countdown

See exactly how many days are left for your target exam.

✏️

Problems of the Day

15 questions by default: 5 each in Physics, Chemistry and Maths. Choose Light (10), Standard (15) or Intensive (30).

⏱️

Mock tests

Timer, question palette, mark for review and automatic scoring, with subject, chapter and time analysis.

🏆

Weekly challenge

A common weekend test with score, rank, percentile and a leaderboard.

📓

Mistake book

Save wrong answers and tag them: concept, calculation, silly, misread, time or guess.

📊

Performance analytics

Find your weak topics and see whether you are improving over time.

🔥

Streaks and achievements

Rewards for real preparation, not for just opening the app.

📚

Resource library

Chapter-wise short notes for Physics, Chemistry and Maths.
